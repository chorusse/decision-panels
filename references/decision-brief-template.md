# Decision brief — output template

Deliver the panel's conclusion in this shape. Keep it tight and scannable — a busy founder or consultant should get the call in the first ten seconds and the reasoning if they read on.

---

## Decision
> _One-sentence statement of the real decision, as reframed in step 1._

**Options considered:** _A · B · C (and "do nothing" if relevant)._
**Stakes / reversibility:** _one line — how consequential, how hard to undo._

## The recommendation
**→ [Chosen option].** Confidence: **[High / Medium / Low]**

_The 2–3 decisive reasons, in order of weight. Rank the alternatives: [A] over [B] over [C], and one line on why each falls where it does._

## Did the panel beat one good answer?

**Solo baseline (taken before the panel convened):** _[recommendation] · confidence [H/M/L] — [the single reason that drove it]._

**Verdict: [Panel changed the call / Panel changed the confidence / Panel added a decisive risk / Panel added nothing].**

_One or two lines: exactly what the panel added, or — if it added nothing — say so plainly: "The panel did not beat the single answer here. You could have asked once." Then one line on what kind of decision **would** have needed a panel._

> _This section is mandatory. A brief without it is incomplete. Decision Panels reports this on every run, including the runs where the answer is unflattering — that is what makes the other runs worth believing._

## The panel
_One line per panelist: name — its position — the crux of its reasoning._
- **[Lens]:** [position] — [why]
- **[Lens]:** [position] — [why]
- _(…5–7 lenses)_

## The real disagreement
_The sharpest genuine split on the panel, both sides steelmanned. This is the part a single opinion would have hidden._

## The strongest case against the recommendation (preserved dissent)
_Stated so a smart skeptic would nod. Do not soften it. If the recommendation is wrong, this is probably why._

## What would change the call
_The specific new information or changed condition that would flip the ranking — i.e. what to go find out before (or instead of) committing._
